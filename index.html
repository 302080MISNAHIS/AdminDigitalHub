WHO WE ARE

About Our School

A small school with a big vision.

Melodias Imperial Sr. National High School is a secondary school serving learners in Barangay Calagonsao, Alcantara, Romblon. Since its establishment in 2017, the school has worked to create a supportive learning environment where every learner can discover strengths, develop skills, and prepare for the future.

Our school community values academic growth, responsible citizenship, creativity, collaboration, sportsmanship, and service.
